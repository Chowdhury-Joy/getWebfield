Our Story

Built In Louisiana. Tested By Every Hurricane Season.

Roof Builders began after Hurricane season showed us how many families were left waiting — on hold with out-of-state call centers, getting upsold on work they didn't need, or watching small leaks turn into full ceiling collapses while they waited for a callback.

We built a different kind of roofing company. One where the owner answers the phone. Where inspections are free and documented with photos you can actually understand. Where repairs are scoped to fix the real problem, not pad an invoice.

Today we serve homeowners and businesses across Greater New Orleans, Baton Rouge, and the Houston metro — with the same local accountability we started with. When you call, you get a Louisiana team that knows your climate, your insurance process, and what it takes for a roof to survive the next storm.

What We Stand For

Four Promises We Make On Every Job

These aren't marketing lines — they're the standards our crew is held to before they step on your property.

Promise 01

Honest Inspections

We photograph every issue, explain it in plain language, and tell you when a repair beats a replacement. If your roof is fine, we'll say so — even if it means we don't get the job.

Promise 02

Storm-Ready Workmanship

Louisiana roofs face heat, humidity, and hurricane-force wind. We install to FORTIFIED standards where it matters and use materials rated for Gulf Coast conditions — not whatever's cheapest.

Promise 03

Local Accountability

We're not a franchise with a 1-800 number. Our office is in Metairie. Our crews live here. When something needs follow-up, the same team that did the work comes back.

Promise 04

Clean Job Sites

Magnetic nail sweeps, daily cleanup, and a final walkthrough with you before we leave. Most full replacements are done in 72 hours — with your yard looking like we were never there.

Credentials

Licensed, Insured, And Certified For Your Peace Of Mind

Roofing is one of the highest-liability trades on your home. We carry full general liability and workers' compensation coverage, maintain active Louisiana and Texas licensing, and train our crews on manufacturer specifications so your warranty stays valid.

We also help navigate insurance claims after storm damage — documenting damage correctly, meeting adjuster timelines, and making sure you understand what's covered before any work starts.

Louisiana & Texas Licensed
