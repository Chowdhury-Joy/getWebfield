## Customer Journeys

### New visitor → free estimate
1. Land on hero — goal: trust + clarity — CTA: Book a Free Estimate / Call
2. Scan about + stats/review — goal: credibility
3. Pick a service card — goal: match need — CTA: service-specific estimate links → `#contact`
4. Optional: process / financing / FAQ — goal: remove objections
5. Submit contact form or call `(973) 513-2992` — goal: lead capture

### Visitor → phone call
1. See top-bar / nav phone or trust ticker
2. Tap Call CTA anywhere on page
3. Speak with Roof Shark — success: booked inspection
