## Customer Journeys

### New visitor → free estimate
1. Land on hero — goal: trust + clarity — CTA: Book a Free Estimate / Call
2. Scan about + stats/review — goal: credibility
3. Pick a service card — goal: match need — CTA: service-specific estimate links → `#contact`
4. Optional: process / financing / FAQ — goal: remove objections
5. Submit contact form or call `(973) 513-2992` — goal: lead capture

### Visitor → phone call
1. See top-bar / nav phone or trust ticker
2. Tap Call CTA anywhere on page
3. Speak with Roof Shark — success: booked inspection

### Custom Timber Buildings preview → quote
1. Hero — goal: "they build anything" — CTA: Get a free quote / Call 01935 891195
2. Price-promise strip — goal: remove the "bespoke must be expensive" objection
3. About + past work — goal: credibility as a manufacturer
4. Pick a building type from the nine cards — CTA: Get my quote → `#contact`
5. Delivery map — goal: confirm they cover the visitor's location
6. Reviews / FAQ — goal: remove remaining objections
7. Submit the form (size, type, requirements, delivery location) or call — goal: lead capture
