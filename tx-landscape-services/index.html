FAQ

Questions we hear before the first visit

Straight answers about coverage, services, and how we work with your property.

Where do you provide landscaping services?

We serve North Dallas–Fort Worth, including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Denton, Prosper, Little Elm, Celina, and surrounding areas.

Do you handle commercial and residential properties?

Yes. We manage commercial grounds for property managers, healthcare facilities, and building owners, plus residential estate landscape care for private properties.

Do you offer standalone landscape design?

We don’t offer design as a standalone service. We collaborate with landscape architects — yours or partners we trust — to support design/build and long-term maintenance success.

What is the TLS Approach?

It’s how we run every program: responsive service, creative plant and color choices suited to Texas, teamwork with your goals, and a consistent, professional image for the property.

Can you help with water and irrigation?

Yes. Water management is part of our work — smart irrigation and water-wise planting so landscapes stay healthy without wasting a limited resource.

Is every maintenance program the same?

No. We customize a program for each property based on site conditions, plant needs, and budget so commercial campuses and estates get the care they actually need.

How do I get a free cost estimate?

Request a quote on this page or call 469-455-9733. Tell us about your property and we’ll walk you through a custom program.
