Faqs

Got questions? We’ve got answers

Straight answers before your first visit.

Where do you provide landscaping services?

We serve North Dallas–Fort Worth, including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Denton, Prosper, Little Elm, Celina, and surrounding areas.

Do you handle commercial and residential properties?

Yes. We work with property management companies, healthcare facilities, building owners, HOAs, and residential estate owners.

Do you offer standalone landscape design?

We don’t offer design as a standalone service. We collaborate with landscape architects — yours or partners we trust — to support design/build and long-term maintenance.

What is the TLS Approach?

Service, creativity, teamwork, and results — a responsive team, traffic-stopping plant and color choices, partnership with your goals, and a consistent professional image for the property.

Can you help with water and irrigation?

Yes. Water management is part of our work — smart irrigation and plant choices suited to Texas conditions.
