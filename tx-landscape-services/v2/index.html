Faqs

Got questions? We’ve got answers

Where do you provide landscaping services?

We serve North Dallas–Fort Worth, including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Denton, Prosper, Little Elm, Celina, and surrounding areas.

Who can use TLS services?

Property managers, healthcare facilities, building owners, HOAs, and private estate homeowners.

Do you offer standalone landscape design?

We don’t offer design as a standalone service. We collaborate with landscape architects — yours or partners we trust.

Does TLS support water-wise landscaping?

Yes. Smart irrigation and water-wise planting are part of how we keep landscapes healthy in Texas.

How can I get started?
